Computational Beha**Vi**or and Heal**T**h **A**na**L**ytics (**ViTAL**) Lab strives to develop **Patient-centered Artificial Intelligence (AI) systems** that are *Scalable*, *Accessible*, and *Ethical* to effectively improve the **Healthcare** system. 
Our mission is to develop **Edge Computing**, **Computer Vision**, and **Machine Learning** systems using distributed ambient, mobile, and wearable devices to monitor patients' conditions in hospitals or everyday life. We are also invested in *deploying* and *testing* the developed AI systems in real-world clinical and daily living environments, actively collaborating with *stakeholders* in healthcare.

| 2026/08/17 | : 🏆 Dr. Kwon is awarded [NIH R01 grant](https://grants.nih.gov/funding/activity-codes/R01) (1R01NS146515-01A1) from [National Institute of Neurological Disorders and Stroke (NINDS)](https://www.ninds.nih.gov/) on “Scalable and Affordable Artificial Intelligence-driven Clinical Decision Support for Movement Disorders”. This *5-year grant* will support developing scalable and accessible AI-driven technologies to objectively assess motor symptoms and mental health in people living with Parkinson’s disease (PD). We will integrate video, wearable technologies, remote interviews, and artificial intelligence to develop clinical decision-support tools that are co-designed with clinicians and engineers and validated in a real-world movement disorders center. We are incredibly fortunate to work with an outstanding interdisciplinary team, including [J. Lucas McKay PhD MSCR](https://jlucasmckay.bmi.emory.edu/global/cv/) (Emory Biomedical Informatics & Neurology), [Christine Esper](https://med.emory.edu/directory/profile/?u=CEDOSS) (Emory Neurology), [Azra Ismail](https://www.azraismail.me/) (Emory Biomedical Informatics & Global Health), [David Anderson](https://people.research.gatech.edu/david-anderson) (Georgia Tech ECE), [Stewart Factor](https://med.emory.edu/directory/profile/?u=SFACTOR) (Emory Neurology), and [Richa Tripathi](https://med.emory.edu/directory/profile/?u=RTRIPA3) (Emory Neurology).
