Master Student, School of Electrical and Computer Engineering, Georgia Institute of Technology

Anton Cobian Iregui is an M.S. student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the Georgia Institute of Technology. He earned his undergraduate degree in Telecommunications Engineering with a concentration in Biomedical Engineering from Universidad Pontificia Comillas (ICAI) in Spain, completing his final year as an exchange student at Georgia Tech. His previous research focused on blind source separation techniques for electromyographic (EMG) signals, with applications in prosthetic control. His research interests include digital signal processing, machine learning, communications, electronics, and biomedical applications, with a particular interest in developing signal processing techniques for healthcare technologies.
