Ph.D. student, Departments of Computer Science, College of Arts and Sciences, Emory University

I am a PhD student in Computer Science and Informatics at Emory University, where I also earned my BS in Mathematics and Computer Science. My research focuses on developing multimodal behavioral AI models for healthcare, combining wearable and ambient sensing to predict behavioral and clinical outcomes in real-world patient populations. My past work includes building data collection pipelines on edge computing devices and regressing clinical assessment scores from motion data in older adults with MCI.
