Ph.D. Student, School of Electrical and Computer Engineering, College of Engineering, Georgia Institute of Technology

Tianwei Jiang is a Ph.D. student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the Georgia Institute of Technology, co-advised by Prof. David V. Anderson and Dr. Hyeokhyen Kwon. Her research focuses on intelligent acoustic perception and multimodal learning, with an emphasis on audio and audio-visual machine learning.
She earned undergraduate degrees in Audio and Music Engineering and Mathematics. Before beginning her Ph.D., she worked with the Audio Lab team at Agora. During her Ph.D., she also interned at Dolby Laboratories, where she worked on deep learning for audio perception and spatial audio algorithms.
